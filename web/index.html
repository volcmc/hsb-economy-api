    Endpoints

    All files live under /v1/. Timestamps are Unix seconds; prices are coins. Full schemas are in the README.

    FileContents
    v1/prices.jsonCheapest current way to buy each item: bazaar instant buy or lowest BIN.
    v1/bazaar.jsonEvery bazaar product with order-book prices, volumes and trends.
    v1/auctions.jsonEvery auctioned item with lowest BIN, listings, sale stats and trends.
    v1/history/bazaar/<id>.jsonHourly (7 days) and daily (1 year) instant buy/sell history.
    v1/history/auctions/<key>.jsonHourly and daily lowest BIN and listing-count history.
    v1/meta.jsonBuild time, source timestamps, counts and sales coverage.
